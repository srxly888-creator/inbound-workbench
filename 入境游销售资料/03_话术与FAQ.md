# 03 · 话术与 FAQ

> 客户在 WhatsApp 上看的第一眼就是你的文字。所有话术先存这里，再复制粘贴，**不要每次现编**。

## 使用规则
- 每次外发前必改：**客户名字 / 具体需求 / 上次沟通内容**
- 不要连发 3 段长文，**一段一段发**，模拟真人聊天
- 用 emoji 适度（😊 🙏 👌 ✈️），别每句都用
- 报价必须等需求确认后才发；**不要凭感觉报价**
- 客户沉默 14 天转低频月触达，不要每天发催

## 一、首响 / 破冰（≤2 小时）

**模板 A · 官网询盘**
> Hi [Name], this is [你名] from China Travel 😊
> Thanks for reaching out about a China trip! To recommend the best options, could you share:
> 1) How many travelers?
> 2) Preferred travel month?
> 3) Which destinations interest you?
> 4) Approximate trip length?
> 5) Any must-see or specific interests (food, history, nature, family)?
> Feel free to send in English or 中文都行 👍

**模板 B · Google Ads / 转介绍**
> Hi [Name], I'm [你名] from China Travel — we design private, customized China journeys with English-speaking local guides, all transfers & hotels included, plus 24/7 support.
> Tell me a bit about your ideal China trip and I'll put together options for you ✈️

**模板 C · 中文客户（少数族裔 / 华裔）**
> 您好 [Name]，我是 China Travel 的 [你名]。看到您对[具体线路]感兴趣，方便告诉我出行人数、时间和想去的地方吗？我们有英文导游全程陪同 👍

## 二、需求五问
1. **人数**（成人 / 儿童 / 老人）— 决定车型、房间数、儿童价
2. **时间**（具体日期 or 月份）— 决定档期与价格
3. **天数**（5 / 10 / 15 天）— 决定线路深度
4. **目的地**（北京 / 西安 / 成都 / …）— 决定主推线路
5. **预算 / 风格**（经济 / 主流 / 高端；文化 / 美食 / 家庭 / 摄影）— 决定方案层级

> 拿到 5 个回答后再报价。**没拿到预算也要报价时**，先给"中位方案"，标注"可根据预算调整"。

- 第 7 天："Hi [Name], the [月份] dates are filling up. Let me know if you'd like to lock in the spot with a small deposit."
- 14 天沉默：转低频月触达

## 五、异议处理（高频）

### "Is China safe?"
> China is generally very safe for travelers. You'll have a private English-speaking guide and driver throughout, plus our 24/7 support line if anything comes up. Most travelers tell us it felt safer than they expected 😊

### "Will WhatsApp / Google work in China?"
> Some Western apps (WhatsApp, Google, Instagram, Facebook) are restricted on the mainland network. We recommend getting a China eSIM or pocket WiFi before arrival so you can stay connected through local apps and a VPN. We send a pre-trip tech guide after booking 👍

### "We need a visa — is it hard?"
> Depends on your passport. Many travelers qualify for the 240-hour visa-free transit (if continuing to a third country). Otherwise a standard L tourist visa is straightforward — usually 4–7 business days at your local Chinese consulate. We share a step-by-step guide after you book. Note: final visa decisions are made by the Chinese authorities.

### "Can you customize the itinerary?"
> Absolutely — that's what we do best. Tell me what you'd like to add, swap, or remove, and I'll send a revised option within 24 hours.

### "Why is the price higher than [other company]?"
> We're a private, fully-tailored service — not a group shopping tour. You get a dedicated guide, private vehicle, vetted 4–5★ hotels, all transfers included, plus 24/7 support. Many travelers tell us the value difference shows up in the experience, not just the line items. Happy to share what's comparable on our end if you want to compare apples to apples.

### "Can we pay less / get a discount?"
> Our pricing already reflects direct supplier rates, no middleman markup. If your dates are flexible I can check if any alternative dates offer better hotel rates. (注：除非主管授权，不要口头承诺折扣)

## 七、FAQ 速查

| 问题 | 一句话答 |
|---|---|
| 几月去最好 | 春秋（4–5、9–10）；张家口夏凉，北方冬季可看雪 |
| 现金要带多少 | 大城市刷卡/移动支付普及；备 $200–500 现金 + 国际信用卡 |
| 小费要吗 | 中国无强制小费；导游司机满意可酌情 |
| 几星酒店 | 默认 4–5 星；可按预算调 |
| 跟团/私人团 | 我们 100% 私人团，非大巴购物团 |
| 餐食含吗 | 多数线路含早餐；午晚餐多不含，可定制 |
| 火车票怎么买 | 我们用护照实名预约（12306），客户无需自抢 |
| 能改行程吗 | 可以，24h 内出修订方案 |
| 中文/英文导游 | 英文为主；按需可安排西/法/德/日等 |

> 这些是"电梯版"答案。客户深问时，回去看 `04_签证 / 01_产品知识库` 的对应条目。
