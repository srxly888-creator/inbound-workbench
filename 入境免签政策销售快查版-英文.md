# China Visa-Free Quick Reference (EN) — for Overseas CS / WhatsApp

> **Purpose**: 30-second answer to *“Does my customer need a visa for China?”* — how long they can stay, what documents they need, which channel to use.
> **Last checked**: 2026-08-17 · **Official sources**: MFA Consular Affairs (mutual list, 2026-05-29) + NIA transit policy (widened 2024-12-17, Indonesia added 2025-06-12).
> **Policies change — always confirm against the latest official notice before quoting/ticketing.**

## TL;DR — Three ways to enter China without a normal tourist visa

| | ① Visa-free entry | ② 240h transit visa-free | ③ L tourist visa |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | No visa at all — just show up | NOT a visa; a transit exemption | A real visa applied for in advance |
| Apply at embassy? | **No** | **No** (done at port) | **Yes** (before departure) |
| Onward ticket needed? | No | **Yes** (China → 3rd country/region) | No |
| Max stay | **≤30 days** | **≤10 days** (240h) | Per visa, usually 30–90 days |
| Where can they go | Nationwide | **24 designated provinces only** (NOT Inner Mongolia, Jilin, Tibet, Gansu, Qinghai, Ningxia, Xinjiang) | Nationwide |
| Cost | Free | Free | Visa fee applies |
| Good for | Most visa-free nationals | “Stop in China a few days en route to a 3rd country” | Anyone not on any visa-free list |

**Key reminder:** ② transit visa-free is **not** a tourist visa. It is free and needs no embassy visit, but it **requires an onward ticket** and **restricts the area**. If a customer can use ①, use ① — it is simpler.

## Decision tree (for any customer's passport)

1. **Is the country in A1 or A2 below?** → **Yes**: ordinary passport, just buy a flight, **visa-free ≤30 days**, no onward ticket needed. ✅ Done.
2. **Not in A1/A2, but in B (the 10 transit-only countries)?** → **Yes**: must buy a ticket *home country → China (stay a few days) → 3rd country*, use 240h transit visa-free, **≤10 days**, within the 24 provinces. ✅ Done.
3. **In B's 55-country transit program but already in A1/A2?** → Just use A1/A2 (simpler, 30 days).
4. **In neither?** → Ordinary passport needs an **L tourist visa** applied for in advance.

## A. Visa-free entry (no visa, no onward ticket) — ≤30 days

### A1. Bilateral visa-free (mutual treaty) — permanent, no expiry

**34 countries** — ordinary passport holders can enter China visa-free for tourism/business/visit/family ≤30 days:

Albania, Antigua and Barbuda, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Bangladesh, Barbados, Belarus, Benin, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Dominica, Ecuador, Fiji, Georgia, Grenada, Kazakhstan, Laos, Malaysia, Maldives, Mauritius, North Macedonia, Qatar, Samoa, San Marino, Serbia, Seychelles, Singapore, Solomon Islands, Suriname, Tajikistan, Thailand, The Bahamas, Tonga, United Arab Emirates, Uzbekistan

### A2. Unilateral visa-free (China grants visa-free entry) — valid through 2026-12-31

**45 countries** — ordinary passport holders can enter China visa-free for tourism/business/visit/family ≤30 days:

Andorra, Argentina, Australia, Austria, Bahrain, Belgium, Brazil, Bulgaria, Chile, Croatia, Cyprus, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Kuwait, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Luxembourg, Malta, Monaco, Montenegro, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Oman, Peru, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Saudi Arabia, Slovakia, Slovenia, South Korea, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Uruguay

> **Overlap note**: Several countries appear in **both A1 and A2** (e.g. UAE, Qatar, Singapore, Thailand, Malaysia, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Serbia, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Belarus, Albania, Armenia, Georgia, North Macedonia). They may use either; **A1 bilateral mutual is permanent**, so prefer it. A2 unilateral policies currently run **through 2026-12-31**.

## B. 240-hour transit visa-free (TWOV) — ≤10 days, needs onward ticket

The transit program actually covers **55 countries** (the 45 in A2 + the 10 below). For A2 nationals transit is redundant (they already get 30 days). The **10 countries below are the ones that can ONLY enter China visa-free via transit** (their ordinary passports are not on any direct visa-free list):

**10 transit-only countries**: Brunei, Canada, Czech Republic, Indonesia, Lithuania, Mexico, Russia, Ukraine, United Kingdom, United States

**Transit rules (all 55 eligible countries)**:
- Ordinary passport valid ≥3 months; **confirmed onward ticket with date/seat** from China to a *different* country/region than the one they came from.
- Enter via one of **60 designated ports** in **24 provinces**; stay counted from **00:00 the day after arrival**, max 10 days.
- Register accommodation within 24 hours of arrival.
- **Not valid** in: Inner Mongolia, Jilin, Tibet, Gansu, Qinghai, Ningxia, Xinjiang.

## C. Need to apply for L tourist visa in advance

If a customer's country is **not** in A1, A2, or B, their ordinary passport needs an **L tourist visa** from a Chinese embassy/consulate before travel.

**Common source countries (examples, 16 listed)**: Colombia, Egypt, India, Iran, Iraq, Israel, Mongolia, Nepal, North Korea, Pakistan, Philippines, South Africa, Sri Lanka, Turkey, Venezuela, Vietnam

> These are the most common inbound-source countries that still need a visa. **Any country not listed in A1/A2/B also needs an L visa** — when in doubt, check the official list or tell the customer to confirm with the nearest Chinese embassy.

## Documents needed per channel

| Channel | Required documents | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ① Visa-free | Valid ordinary passport (recommend ≥6 months), return/onward flight (may be checked), hotel booking, proof of funds | No visa, no onward-ticket requirement |
| ② Transit | Passport (≥3 months), **confirmed onward ticket China→3rd country (date/seat fixed)**, itinerary/accommodation | Apply for transit at the port, free |
| ③ L visa | Passport, visa form, photo, itinerary, hotel+flight bookings, proof of funds (invitation letter sometimes) | Apply at Chinese embassy, visa fee, lead time needed |

## WhatsApp / CS reply scripts (copy-paste)

**Q1: Do I need a visa for China?**
> It depends on your passport. If you hold a passport from [list major visa-free: France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Switzerland, Ireland, Hungary, Austria, Belgium, Luxembourg, Poland, Portugal, Greece, the Nordics, South Korea, Japan, Australia, New Zealand, Thailand, Singapore, Malaysia, UAE, Qatar, Brazil, Argentina, etc. — see full A1/A2 lists], ordinary-passport tourists can enter **visa-free for up to 30 days**. The US, Canada, UK, Russia and a few others can use the **240-hour transit visa-free** (needs an onward ticket to a third country). Everyone else needs a tourist (L) visa in advance.

**Q2: How long can I stay?**
> Visa-free / mutual: usually **up to 30 days**. Transit visa-free: **up to 10 days** (240 hours). Longer stays require a residence permit or visa.

**Q3: How does the transit visa-free work?**
> Book a connecting itinerary *your country → China (stay a few days) → a third country/region*. At a Chinese port of entry, use the 240-hour transit channel with your confirmed onward ticket, enter via one of the 60 designated ports, and travel within the allowed regions for ≤10 days.

**Q4: Can I work or study on visa-free entry?**
> No. Visa-free entry covers tourism, business, visiting and family. Work, study or journalism require the correct visa obtained in advance.

**Q5: My country isn't on the visa-free list — what now?**
> You'll need to apply for an L tourist visa at the nearest Chinese embassy/consulate before you travel (passport, photo, itinerary, hotel+flight bookings, proof of funds). We can help you prepare the documents.

## Risks & reminders (always tell the customer)

- Policies adjust — **verify against the latest official notice before quoting or ticketing**.
- Unilateral visa-free (A2) currently runs **through 2026-12-31**; whether it extends is unknown.
- Transit visa-free **requires a real onward ticket** to a different country/region than the origin — otherwise entry is refused.
- Register accommodation within 24h of arrival; overstaying or leaving the allowed provinces is treated as illegal stay and harms future entries.
- **Passport type matters**: for some countries (e.g. Brunei, Saudi Arabia) mutual visa-free covers **diplomatic/service passports only** — ordinary-passport holders use A2 (Saudi) or B (Brunei).
